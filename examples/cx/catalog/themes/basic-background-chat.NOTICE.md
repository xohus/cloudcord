# BasicBackground (Chat)

CloudCord adapted the chat wallpaper, accent, and text colors from [BasicBackground by DevilBro](https://betterdiscord.app/themes/BasicBackground). This edition applies the image to Discord chat on both clients. The original theme also styles other Discord screens.

The [pinned BetterDiscord source](https://github.com/mwittrien/BetterDiscordAddons/blob/bca7c871d2374f0b21e836d60dbf096d65df53aa/Themes/BasicBackground/BasicBackground.theme.css) is distributed under the [upstream GPL-2.0 license](https://github.com/mwittrien/BetterDiscordAddons/blob/bca7c871d2374f0b21e836d60dbf096d65df53aa/LICENSE). The [original wallpaper](https://github.com/mwittrien/BetterDiscordAddons/blob/bca7c871d2374f0b21e836d60dbf096d65df53aa/Themes/BasicBackground/_res/background.jpg) includes a notice permitting free use. The bundled wallpaper was resized and re-encoded for mobile delivery.
